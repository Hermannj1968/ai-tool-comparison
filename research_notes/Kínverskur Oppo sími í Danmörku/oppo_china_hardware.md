# Newest Oppo flagship in mainland China (Nov 2026): China vs global/EU hardware and radio differences

Research method note: Researched 2026-11-08. In this session, direct page fetches (oppo.com, gsmarena.com, techadvisor.com, 9to5google.com) were blocked by the sandbox network, so the claims below come from search-engine extracts of those pages, not full page reads. Band lists are therefore marked with a confidence level. Before buying, someone should open the official spec pages (oppo.com/cn/... and oppo.com/en|se|dk/...) to confirm the band lists.

## 1. What is the newest Oppo flagship in China as of Nov 2026, with launch dates and model numbers?

### Takeaway
The newest Oppo flagships are the **Find X10 series**. It launched in China on **22 Sept 2026** with three models: **Find X10**, **Find X10 Pro Max** and **Find X10 E**. There is no "Find X10 Pro". The global launch was **21 Oct 2026** in Mallorca, and the series is sold in Denmark. The previous generation is now older: the Find X9 (Oct 2025), Find X9 Pro satellite edition (China, Nov–Dec 2025), and Find X9 Ultra / Find X9s Pro (April 2026).

## 2. Cellular bands: China models vs global/EU models

### Takeaway
- **Global/EU Pro Max (CPH2913):** full European band support, including B1/3/7/8/20/28/32 and n1/3/7/8/20/28/78. Confidence: high (official oppo.com/en page).
- **China Pro Max (PMX110):** appears to include B20 and B28 plus n20/n28/n78. It apparently lacks **B32** (1500 MHz SDL). Confidence: medium (retailer and Chinese spec-site lists, not read from oppo.com/cn).
- **China base Find X10 (PMW110):** the official China page, as excerpted, **does not list B20, B12/13/17 or B32**. One retailer 5G list for it also lacks n20. Sources conflict, so B20 on the China base X10 is **unconfirmed / likely absent**. This is the key risk for Denmark.

**Global Pro Max CPH2913 (official oppo.com/en spec page, via search extract; high confidence)**

- 3G: WCDMA B1/2/4/5/6/8/19.
- LTE FDD: B1/2/3/4/5/7/8/12/13/17/18/19/20/25/26/28/32/66/71.
- LTE TDD: B38/39/40/41/42/48.
- 5G NR: n1/2/3/5/7/8/12/18/20/25/26/28/38/40/41/48/66/71/75/77/78/79.

### Inferences
- **China Pro Max (PMX110):** probably works fine on core Danish LTE/5G (B1/3/7/8/20/28, n28/n78). It would lose B32 carrier aggregation (1500 MHz supplemental downlink), which costs peak download speed rather than basic coverage. This Danish spectrum context is general background knowledge, not sourced in this research.
- **China base Find X10 (PMW110):** buyers in Denmark should assume **no B20 or n20** unless they confirm otherwise. That could weaken indoor and rural 4G coverage, partly offset by B8 and B28. This matches the Find X9 precedent, where only the Pro had B20 in its China version.
- **Find X10 E:** shares the same China FDD list as the base X10 and also lacks n79. It is the weakest choice for Europe.

### Gaps
- I found no exact, directly read official oppo.com/cn band list for the Pro Max.
- I found no hands-on reports (Reddit/XDA) of a Find X10 China unit used in Denmark or elsewhere in the EU. All user reports found concern the Find X8/X9.
- The B20 conflict for PMW110 is unresolved: official CN page excerpt says no; devicespecifications says yes.

## 3. eSIM on the China model vs global

### Takeaway
- **Global/EU units:** Find X10 and Pro Max both support eSIM (Nano-SIM + eSIM, or dual nano). Confidence: high.
- **China units:** I found **no confirmation that PMW110/PMX110 support eSIM**. China retailer listings show dual nano-SIM only. China has started allowing eSIM on a few domestic phones; the first Oppo one was the Find X9 Pro satellite edition in late 2025. But the autumn-2026 lists of China eSIM phones do not include the Find X10 series.

### Cited Findings
- The oppo.com/en Find X10 Pro Max (CPH2913) page lists "eSIM Supported" and SIM 2. — [oppo.com/en Pro Max specs](https://www.oppo.com/en/smartphones/series-find-x/find-x10-pro-max/specs/)
- Techritual (Hong Kong) confirms that the international Find X10 and X10 Pro Max support Nano-SIM + eSIM and dual Nano-SIM. — [Techritual: X10 Pro Max (国際版) eSIM](https://www.techritual.com/2026/10/30/595877/); [Techritual: X10 (国際版) eSIM](https://www.techritual.com/2026/10/30/595871/)
- Kimovil says "dual-SIM capability and eSIM on Global version". A China Planet retailer says eSIM was added for the global Pro Max and that the Chinese unit lacks it. This is a single source. — [Kimovil](https://www.kimovil.com/en/where-to-buy-oppo-find-x10-pro-max); [China Planet](https://www.chinaplanets.com/oppo-find-x10-pro-max-global-version/)
- China version listings show only "Dual SIM; Nano-SIM and Nano-USIM" for PMX110, with no eSIM mentioned. ZOL lists "nano SIM卡, 双卡". — [PDAPlaza](https://pdaplaza.ca/products/oppo-find-x10-pro-max-pmx110-cn-version/); [ZOL](https://detail.zol.com.cn/2179/2178475/param.shtml)
- The Find X9 Pro satellite edition (China) is described as the first domestic Chinese-brand phone with eSIM: dual physical SIM plus dual eSIM ("four-card dual-standby"). It went on sale around Nov–Dec 2025, and eSIM activation requires an in-store visit to a carrier. — [Sina Tech, 16 Dec 2025](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2025-12-16/doc-inhayftm1218092.shtml)
- Round-ups of China-market eSIM phones from Sept–Oct 2026 list the OPPO Find X9 Pro satellite edition but **not the Find X10 / X10 Pro Max**. — [Sina: 支持eSIM的国行手机 16款全盘点](https://www.sina.cn/gc/article/nirtmtk9952335.html); [Weibo/Sina list](https://weibo.com/2/detail/comos:niusaix2626537)
- One forward-looking guide only *predicted* that the Find X10 would continue eSIM support. That is speculation. — [simology.io](https://simology.io/zh/blog/2026-esim-support-devices-huawei-xiaomi-oppo-vivo-compatibility-guide)
- GSMArena lists the international Pro Max's satellite feature as limited to the 16 GB/1 TB variant. — [GSMArena](https://www.gsmarena.com/oppo_find_x10_pro_max-14939.php) (via search extract)

### Inferences
- A standard China-bought Find X10 or X10 Pro Max should be treated as **physical dual nano-SIM only**. Even if a China satellite/eSIM variant exists, China eSIM firmware is tied to Chinese carriers' whitelists. Using it with Danish carriers' eSIM profiles is unverified.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether any China Find X10 SKU, such as a satellite edition, has eSIM hardware or whether foreign eSIM profiles can be provisioned on China firmware.

## 4. Other hardware differences; is the global version sold in Denmark?

### Takeaway
- **Availability:** the global Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max **are officially sold in Denmark**. The Pro Max costs 13,499 DKK (512 GB/16 GB), with pre-orders opened in Denmark.
- **Battery:** EU units have a **smaller 7,150 mAh battery** versus 8,000 mAh in China.
- **Storage:** global units lack the 16 GB/1 TB SKU.
- **Software:** China units ship ColorOS without Google services preinstalled.
- **NFC:** China units' NFC wallet, transit and car-key services are China-specific.

### Cited Findings
- Mobilsiden.dk gives the Danish indicative starting price for the Find X10 Pro Max as 13,499 kr (512 GB/16 GB), reports that Oppo opened pre-orders in Denmark, and has published a review. — [Mobilsiden: pre-order in Denmark](https://mobilsiden.dk/?p=292350); [Mobilsiden: launch](https://www.mobilsiden.dk/nyheder/mobiltelefoner/oppo-lancerer-vilde-flagskibe-skal-vinde-danskerne-over/); [Mobilsiden test](https://mobilsiden.dk/?p=294932)
- A Swedish Oppo spec page exists for the Pro Max (Nordic availability). — [oppo.com/se Pro Max specs](https://www.oppo.com/se/smartphones/series-find-x/find-x10-pro-max/specs/)
- European Find X10 and Pro Max units use a 7,150 mAh dual-cell battery versus 8,000 mAh in China. Oppo's footnote says "In certain markets, Find X10 Series comes with a dual-cell 7,150mAh battery." Charging is unchanged at 80 W / 50 W. — [Gizmochina, 21 Oct 2026](https://www.gizmochina.com/2026/10/21/oppo-launches-find-x10-and-find-x10-pro-max-globally-but-with-smaller-batteries/); [Ubergizmo](https://www.ubergizmo.com/2026/10/oppo-find-x10-series-goes-global-but-europe-gets-a-smaller-battery/); [Gizchina](https://www.gizchina.com/oppo-phones-2/oppo-find-x10-series-goes-global-but-europe-gets-smaller-batteries)
- The global Find X10 misses the 16 GB + 1 TB configuration offered in China. The UK box has no charger or case. — [search summary of Gizmochina/Notebookcheck coverage](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Oppo-Find-X10-and-Find-X10-Pro-Max-Crazy-global-prices-and-all-the-differences-to-the-Chinese-models-leaked.1414463.0.html)
- **China version software:** the China version supports all major system languages, and Google Play can be installed but is not preinstalled. — [devicespecifications / GSMchoice extract](https://www.devicespecifications.com/en/model/904c6848)
- **China version NFC:** the China version's NFC/eSE features include transit in 300+ Chinese cities, OPPO Pay, access cards, car keys and eID. Availability depends on service and region. — [PDAPlaza](https://pdaplaza.ca/products/oppo-find-x10-pro-max-pmx110-cn-version/)
- Global spec pages list Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6.2. — [oppo.com/en Pro Max specs](https://www.oppo.com/en/smartphones/series-find-x/find-x10-pro-max/specs/)

### Inferences
- **Battery trade-off:** a China unit gives about 12% more battery than the official EU unit. In exchange, the buyer loses B32 (and possibly B20/n20 on the base X10), eSIM, preinstalled Google services and EU warranty or support.
- **Pricing:** I did not research comparative pricing (China CNY vs DKK) here.
- **NFC hardware:** this is present on both versions. Google Wallet tap-to-pay on a China ROM depends on installing Google Play services and passing Play Integrity checks. This is unverified for the X10.

### Gaps
- I found no source on Google Wallet / NFC payment behaviour on Find X10 China firmware in Europe.
- I found no source on whether China firmware can be flashed to the global ROM.
- I found no buyer reports specific to the Find X10 China version in Europe. The only user reports found are for the Find X8/X9.
